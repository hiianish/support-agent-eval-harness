# Standard Shipping to the United States

**Effective May 1, 2026.** This page describes standard shipping to the United States.

## Cost
Standard shipping to the United States costs $5.99. It is free when your order subtotal is $50 or more, after discounts and before tax.

## Delivery time
Orders ship within 1 business day of being placed. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days after the order ships.

## Carriers
UPS or USPS delivers US orders, chosen automatically based on the destination.

## Tracking
A tracking number is emailed as soon as the order ships. Tracking can take up to 12 hours to show its first scan.

## Taxes and duties
Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on the delivery address. No customs charges apply.

## Delivery attempts
The carrier makes up to 3 delivery attempts. If delivery fails, the package is held at a local depot for a few days and then returned to us. We refund returned undelivered orders, minus the original shipping charge.
