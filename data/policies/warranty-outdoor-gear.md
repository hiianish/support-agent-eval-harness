# Warranty: Outdoor Gear

**Effective February 10, 2026.** This warranty applies to outdoor gear sold by Brightwell Market.

## Coverage period
Outdoor gear are covered against manufacturing defects for **24 months** from the delivery date.

## What is covered
Defects in materials or workmanship that affect how the product works, such as failed seams, broken zips, faulty buckles, or frames that crack under normal use.

## What is not covered
Normal wear to fabrics, fading from sunlight, damage from fire or sharp objects, and use outside the product's stated conditions are not covered.

## Making a claim
Follow the warranty claims process. Keep your order record, which serves as proof of purchase.

## Remedy
We choose between repairing, replacing or refunding the product. Replacements are covered for the remainder of the original period.

## Your legal rights
This warranty is in addition to any rights you have under consumer law where you live.
