# Warranty: Home and Kitchen

**Effective February 10, 2026.** This warranty applies to home and kitchen products sold by Brightwell Market.

## Coverage period
Home and kitchen products are covered against manufacturing defects for **36 months** from the delivery date.

## What is covered
Defects in materials or workmanship that affect how the product works, such as motors that stop working, handles that detach, or non-stick coatings that peel under normal use.

## What is not covered
Damage from dishwashers on items not labelled dishwasher-safe, scratches from metal utensils, and misuse beyond the stated capacity are not covered.

## Making a claim
Follow the warranty claims process. Keep your order record, which serves as proof of purchase.

## Remedy
We choose between repairing, replacing or refunding the product. Replacements are covered for the remainder of the original period.

## Your legal rights
This warranty is in addition to any rights you have under consumer law where you live.
