# Two-Day Shipping

**Effective May 10, 2026.** This page describes two-day shipping, available on US orders only.

## Cost
Two-day shipping costs $14.95, charged once per order.

## Order cut-off
Orders placed before 2:00 pm Eastern Time on a business day ship that same day. Orders placed later ship the next business day.

## Delivery
Orders arrive 2 business days after they ship. Available Monday to Friday.

## Not available for
Expedited shipping is not available for PO boxes, Canadian addresses, or pre-orders that have not shipped.

## Changing to expedited
You can upgrade an order to this service before it ships by contacting support with the order number. We will charge the difference in shipping cost.

## Delays
Weather and carrier delays are outside our control. We do not offer a refund of the shipping charge for carrier delays.
